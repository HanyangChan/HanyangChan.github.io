Comparative Analysis of Adaptive MARL Strategies in Non-Stationary Financial Markets

Minju Yang, Byungchan Hwang, Dogyeom Kim, Jinsoo Kim, Minwoo You, Subin Lim

Proceedings of the KIIT Summer Conference 2026 · 5 pages

Multi-Agent Reinforcement LearningMulti-Armed BanditNon-Stationary Market

Abstract

We compare the adaptability and survival strategies of 57 algorithm variants in a non-stationary market constructed from Walmart sales data. The environment models a “density paradox,” in which rewards decrease as agents crowd into the same asset. We evaluate 100,000 simulation episodes using a TrueSkill-based relative rating system.

In Table 1, Decaying Epsilon-Greedy achieves a score of 89.72 and finishes in the top 30% in 79.69% of episodes. The results highlight the benefits of early exploration, adaptive exploration–exploitation transitions, and collision avoidance in this environment.
